03 / Behind the camera

Hello, I'm
MamaLens.

I'm a sports photographer drawn to the energy of competition, the character of the people in it, and the fleeting details that can tell a much bigger story.

My approach is observant and human. I look for the expression before the celebration, the quiet before the whistle, and the moments that reveal what a game or a gathering means to the people there.

This portfolio is a home for the work and the stories behind it. I'm always interested in working with athletes, teams, brands, publications, and people with a story worth telling.

See you out there.
