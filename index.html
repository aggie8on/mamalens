03 / The person behind the camera

Hi, I'm MamaLens.

I'm a photographer drawn to the energy of sport, the honesty of a portrait, and the little details that make a story feel human.

I work with natural curiosity and a documentary eye: watching closely, finding the light, and waiting for the moment when everything comes together. Whether it's on the pitch, in a studio, or out in the world, I'm interested in photographs that feel real.

This space is a living record of the work and the people I get to meet along the way. Have a project in mind, or a story that deserves to be told? I'd love to hear about it.
