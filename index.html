Notes on photography, people and the stories behind the images.

Photography is about more than the final frame. It is also about noticing the people, preparation, movement and small details that give a moment meaning.

This space will bring together stories from MamaLens assignments, creative process notes and highlights from the people and communities in front of the camera.

About Me

Video coming soon

I’m the photographer behind MamaLens. I use photography to pay attention to people, movement and the small details that can turn an ordinary moment into a lasting image.

My work spans sports, portraits, products, studio sessions, outdoor and indoor photography, and events. Each setting asks for a different kind of attention, from anticipating action to finding a thoughtful composition.

This portfolio brings those different kinds of work together. If you have a project, an event or an idea you would like to explore, get in touch and tell me what you have in mind.

Terms & Conditions

By contacting MamaLens or commissioning photography, you agree that project details, deliverables, timelines, fees and usage rights should be confirmed in writing before work begins.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, photographs remain the photographer’s work and may not be copied, resold, altered or used beyond the agreed purpose without permission. Any permitted usage, credit requirements, delivery format and publication dates should be confirmed for each project.

Clients are responsible for obtaining any permissions they need for people, locations, products or materials they ask to be photographed. Every project is subject to availability and a mutually agreed brief.

For questions about a booking or the use of images, please contact MamaLens before using or distributing the photographs.
